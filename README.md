**Solmar Heat** — a complete, original 3D open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built with three.js and playable offline in a desktop browser. You are Rico Vane, a wheelman fresh out of the Solmar docks with $40 and a debt to settle, working jobs for fixer Marisol Vega in the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar.

Explore the city on foot (WASD + mouse look) or by car (arcade driving), steal parked cars, dodge traffic and pedestrians, and outrun the Solmar Police across a 1–5 star wanted system with chasing police cars and foot officers. Five story missions — a tutorial drive, a timed delivery, a car theft and police escape, a dockside shootout, and a final cross-city chase — pay out cash and unlock free-roam play, with pistol combat, a HUD (health, money, wanted stars, speed, mission objective + timer, live 2D minimap), start / pause / busted / wasted screens, synthesized WebAudio sound (engine, gunfire, sirens, jingles), and localStorage saves. Every city, character, vehicle, name and story is original; nothing is taken from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
